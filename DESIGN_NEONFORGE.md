# NeonForge Design DNA (Concept 2)
Source: Google Stitch Project ID `13407078511746843194`
Title: Cyberpunk Enthusiast & Custom Liquid-Cooling Battlestation Showroom

## 1. Aesthetic Thesis: Underground Cyberpunk Battlestation & Liquid Artisan
High-octane visual identity engineered for extreme PC builders, modders, and custom liquid-cooling artisans. Combines high-contrast true-black OLED expanses with frosted glassmorphic containers (16px blur), hairline laser edge illumination, and telemetry matrices. Surfaces evoke anodized aluminum, tinted tempered glass, distilled coolant conduits, and glowing addressable RGB headers.

## 3. Typography
- **Headlines:** `Chakra Petch` / `Space Grotesk`, sans-serif (CNC-machined hardware badges, uppercase tracking)
- **Body:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif (High-contrast readability on true black)
- **Telemetry & Pricing:** `Rajdhani` / `JetBrains Mono`, monospace (Tabular figures, flow rates, MHz, voltage)

## 4. Optical Depth & Elevation
- **Layer 0 (Void):** Absolute background in Deep Space Obsidian `#0A0A0F`, 24px isometric vector micro-grid at 4% opacity.
- **Layer 1 (Sub-Chassis):** Structural bays in Matte Charcoal `#12121A` with 1px hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Layer 2 (Tempered Glass Tiles):** Frosted glass surfaces `rgba(22, 22, 34, 0.75)` with 16px blur.
- **Photonic Edge Glows:**
  - Rest state: `1px solid rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.18)`
  - Active/Hover: `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.65)` with `box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25), inset 0 0 8px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.1)`
  - Warning/Overclock: `rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.35)`
