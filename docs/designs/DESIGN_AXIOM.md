# Concept 3: Axiom Pro — Minimalist Enterprise Workstation & Studio Lab
## Google Stitch Project ID: 14489479806179368582

### 1. Design DNA & Philosophy
- **Aesthetic Direction**: Dieter Rams functional architectural minimalism, clean room industrial design, high information density, structural hairline borders (1px titanium), zero neon blur or gaming tropes.
- **Form Factor**: Enterprise workstation towers, 4U rackmount nodes, dual sTR5 / LGA4677 compute clusters, multi-GPU Ada accelerators.
- **Target Audience**: AI researchers, LLM training labs, DaVinci Resolve color grading studios, AEC / BIM architects (SolidWorks, Revit, Catia), quantitative finance desks.

### 2. Color Tokens
- **Canvas / Background**: `#FBFBFD` (Off-white architectural paper)
- **Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure clinical white)
- **Basalt Charcoal**: `#18181B` (Zinc-900 high contrast dark accents & typography)
- **Primary Brand**: `#004F32` (Deep Forest Emerald — industrial precision)
- **Primary Hover**: `#003823` (Heavy Pine)
- **Accent / Highlight**: `#2563EB` (Precision Cobalt Blue for telemetry, interconnects, and bus lines)
- **Hairline Border**: `#E4E4E7` (Zinc-200 titanium hairline grid)
- **Muted Steel**: `#71717A` / `#52525B` (Zinc-500/600 secondary metadata)
- **Soft Tint / Badge**: `#F4F4F5` (Zinc-100) & `#ECFDF5` (Emerald-50)

### 3. Typography Hierarchy
- **Headings & Structural Titles**: Inter / General Sans / SF Pro Display (`font-sans`, weights 600, 700, 800)
- **Body & Editorial Copy**: Inter (`font-sans`, weights 400, 500, leading relaxed)
- **Parametric Data, Specs, & Metrics**: IBM Plex Mono / JetBrains Mono (`font-mono`, tabular numbers, uppercase tags)

### 4. Component Rules
- **Radii**: Strict 2px to 4px (`rounded-sm` or `rounded`), sharp architectural grid.
- **Borders**: Continuous 1px hairline `border-[#E4E4E7]` separating all modules, tables, and inspection panels.
- **Shadows**: Very soft, subtle diffuse ambient occlusion (`shadow-[0_1px_3px_rgba(0,0,0,0.05)]`) or flat bordered cards.
- **Visual Rhythm**: High density tabular grids, split-screen hero with 3D matte clay viewer, parametric sliders/meters for PCIe lanes and thermal envelopes.
