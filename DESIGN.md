## Brand & Style
This design system serves a technical, no-nonsense hardware audience: system builders, enterprise IT engineers, overclockers, and parametric component shoppers. The visual aesthetic fuses industrial engineering schematic precision with high-efficiency e-commerce architecture. 

Rather than leaning into decorative consumer fluff, the interface adopts a **Precision Technical Brutalism & High-Density Modernist** style:
- **Structural Integrity:** Ultra-crisp hairline dividers, orthographic panel grids, and high data-density layouts inspired by technical spec sheets and parametric search engines.
- **Parametric Scannability:** Immediate visibility of critical telemetry (die architecture, TDP, socket revisions, memory timings, and branch stock tiers) without requiring deep drill-downs.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Uncompromising precision, industrial reliability, high speed, and absolute stock transparency across multi-warehouse logistics.

## Colors
The palette leverages high-contrast optical clarity against an engineering-grade slate spectrum.

- **Primary Action (`#EF4444` / Hover `#DC2626` / Token `#B61722`):** High-Performance Crimson Red. Strictly allocated to high-intent conversion moments (Add to Cart, Buy Now, Configure Build, and active parametric filter pills).
- **Secondary / Deep Slate (`#0F172A` / Token `#565E74`):** Deep Midnight Slate. Used for dominant typography, structural anchors, and active header toolbars.
- **Tertiary / Operational Emerald (`#10B981` / Token `#006947`):** Validation & Availability Green. Signals socket/chassis cross-compatibility, active inventory clearance, and real-time operational status.
- **Neutral Core (`#475569`):** Muted Titanium. Applied to technical attribute labels, unit dimensions, and secondary descriptors.
- **Surfaces & Borders:** 
  - Primary Canvas: Pure Optical White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Alternating Structural Panels: Muted Cool Gray (`#F8FAFC`).
  - Hairline Component Borders: Neutral Slate (`#E2E8F0`).
  - Interactive/Hover Borders: Structural Slate (`#CBD5E1`).
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - Caution Amber (`#F59E0B`): Branch low-stock warnings (< 3 units) and critical wattage thresholds.
  - Direct Sales Green (`#25D366`): Targeted instant-quote and branch engineering assistance via WhatsApp.

## Typography
The typographic architecture separates macro taxonomy, functional parsing, and numeric telemetry:

1. **Space Grotesk (Headlines):** Imparts an industrial, precision-machined tone. Applied strictly in semi-bold weight with tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em`) across category headers, product titles, and configuration modals.
2. **Inter (Body & Controls):** Anchors visual hierarchy at a dense 13px base (`body-md`) with 18px line-height, allowing massive quantities of peripheral specifications to sit neatly on a single viewport without visual fatigue.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Technical Spec-Pills & Pricing):** Ensures tabular, monospace alignment for fast vertical scanning. Pricing prefixes (`৳`), SKU numbers, pinouts (e.g., `AM5`, `LGA1851`), power envelopes (`120W TDP`), and memory clocks (`DDR5-6000MT/s`) are bound to this font to guarantee absolute character alignment across tabular layouts.
